Skellefteå is not its own region on meshcoresverige.se today. Skellefteå is in Västerbotten County, where
the county's values apply:

{{< lan-area "24" >}}

The map has its own area for Skellefteå (SFT), and the traffic from there shows on the map for all of
Sweden. The region for the radios is still Västerbotten's.

Today the regions follow the counties. An area like Skellefteå can get its own subregion within the county if the
people building the mesh there want one: contact louie on [Sweden Mesh Discord](https://discord.gg/gchSzHkPGG) and we'll talk about it.

Read more about how the regions work in [Regions](/guider/regioner/), or start with
[Get started with MeshCore](/kom-igang/).
