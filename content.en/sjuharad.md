Sjuhärad is not its own region on meshcoresverige.se today. Sjuhärad, with Borås, is in Västra Götaland County, where the county's values apply:

{{< lan-area "14" >}}

Today the regions follow the counties. An area like Sjuhärad can get its own subregion within the county if the people
building the mesh there want one: contact louie on [Sweden Mesh Discord](https://discord.gg/gchSzHkPGG) and we'll talk about it.

Read more about how the regions work in [Regions](/guider/regioner/), or start with
