Gothenburg is not its own region on meshcoresverige.se today. Gothenburg is in Västra Götaland County, where the county's values apply:

{{< lan-area "14" >}}

Today the regions follow the counties. An area like Gothenburg can get its own subregion within the county if the people
building the mesh there want one: contact louie on [Sweden Mesh Discord](https://discord.gg/gchSzHkPGG) and we'll talk about it.
