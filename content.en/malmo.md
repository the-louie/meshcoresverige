Malmö is not its own region on meshcoresverige.se today. Malmö is in Skåne County, where the county's values apply:

{{< lan-area "12" >}}

Today the regions follow the counties. An area like Malmö can get its own subregion within the county if the people
building the mesh there want one: contact louie on [Sweden Mesh Discord](https://discord.gg/gchSzHkPGG) and we'll talk about it.

Read more about how the regions work in [Regions](/guider/regioner/), or start with
