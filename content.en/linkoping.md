Linköping is not its own region on meshcoresverige.se today. Linköping is in Östergötland County, where the county's values apply:

Today the regions follow the counties. An area like Linköping can get its own subregion within the county if the people
building the mesh there want one: contact louie on [Sweden Mesh Discord](https://discord.gg/gchSzHkPGG) and we'll talk about it.
