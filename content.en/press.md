## What they cover {#vad-de-handlar-om}

TV4's article
: About community radio networks being built in northern Sweden as part of digital preparedness, and how they work.

TV4's video
: About volunteers building radio networks meant to work without the usual infrastructure; TV4 Nyheterna
  visited Robertsfors in Västerbotten.

Västerbottningen and Västerbottens Mellanbygd
: The same article in both papers: volunteers are building a MeshCore mesh from Nordmaling to south of
  Lövånger, with solar-powered repeaters, that carries text messages without a mobile network or internet.

Ölands Radioamatörer's video
: A recorded talk about MeshCore from the club's Radio Saturday (Radiolördag), about LoRa, the ISM band and how it differs
  from Meshtastic.

## For journalists {#för-journalister}

What MeshCore is: [What is MeshCore?](/guider/vad-ar-meshcore/). What the mesh can do when the power and

any of the groups.
