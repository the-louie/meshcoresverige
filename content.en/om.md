{{< domain >}} is the site for **MeshCore in Sweden**. It brings together guides and wizards for the whole country,
and when you pick your county it shows the county's region (scope), channels, map and places where the mesh
needs repeaters. If the county has its own MeshCore site, we link to it.

The map is built from what the observers around the country hear, through a
[Beacon server](https://beacon.meshcore-jkp.se/swagger/index.html).

## Why the site exists {#varför-sidan-finns}

The guides and wizards are kept up to date in one place, and the regional sites use the same text.
The site is built on the template
[meshcore-regional-template](https://github.com/sweden-mesh/meshcore-regional-template).

## County details {#länens-uppgifter}

Each county has its own data file in
[the-louie/meshcoresverige](https://github.com/the-louie/meshcoresverige) under `data/lan/`. You can easily
contribute more information (channels, groups, places where a repeater is needed), or fix something that
is wrong, with a pull request; how is described in
[CONTRIBUTING.md](https://github.com/the-louie/meshcoresverige/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md).

## Who runs the site {#vem-sköter-sidan}
